# PGI Saathi - User Validation & Usability Protocol

## 1. Study Purpose & Ethical Safeguards

To validate that PGI Saathi is intuitive and safe for real hospital visitors at PGIMER Chandigarh without collecting medical histories, clinical records, or personal data.

### Ethical Boundaries:
- **No Patient Health Information**: Participants must NEVER be asked to disclose their medical condition, CR number, diagnosis, or identity.
- **Simulated Scenarios**: All evaluation tasks use standardized hypothetical visitor journeys (e.g., "Imagine your relative needs an eye checkup; find where to go").
- **Voluntary & Anonymous**: Participants may decline or withdraw at any point.

---

## 2. Standardized Evaluation Tasks

| Task ID | Objective | Scenario Given to Participant | Success Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **T-01** | **Find Specialist Clinic** | "You need to find where the Eye Clinic (AEC) is located." | Participant searches "Eye" or selects "Eye (AEC)" chip; identifies Advanced Eye Centre as a separate building in < 45 seconds. |
| **T-02** | **Emergency Escalation** | "Imagine a person nearby has collapsed with severe chest pain." | Participant searches "chest pain" or taps Emergency; immediately recognizes the Red Alert banner and states they should go to Nehru Hospital Emergency rather than OPD lines. |
| **T-03** | **Language Switching** | "Switch the application language to Hindi or Punjabi." | Participant taps language toggle in header and confirms they can read guidance in their preferred language. |
| **T-04** | **Offline Orientation** | "Your phone is in airplane mode; can you still check the OPD registration hours?" | Participant opens OPD timings card while offline and reads 8:00 AM – 11:00 AM. |
| **T-05** | **Visit Checklist Preparation** | "Mark off ID proof and cash before leaving home." | Participant checks items in checklist and confirms progress indicator increases. |
