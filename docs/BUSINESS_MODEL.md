# PGI Saathi - Sustainable Business Model & Monetization Roadmap

## 1. Executive Summary

PGI Saathi is an independent patient navigation application solving the problem of spatial and procedural disorientation at PGIMER Chandigarh. 

Monetizing a healthcare navigation product requires a strict separation between **unrestricted public patient welfare** and **value-added B2B enterprise / convenience services**. Direct monetization of sick patients (e.g., paywalls, pop-up ads, or data brokering) destroys credibility and exposes the platform to regulatory and reputational liabilities.

This roadmap details an ethical, highly lucrative multi-stage business model centered on **B2B hospital licensing, hyperlocal patient logistics, on-ground concierge support, and CSR grants**.

---

## 2. Core Ethical Guardrails (What We Will NEVER Do)

To maintain absolute patient trust and comply with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act)** and National Medical Commission (NMC) regulations:

1. **Zero Display Banner Ads (No AdSense / Pop-ups)**: No ads promoting unverified treatments, alternative medicine, or private clinics will ever be displayed inside the application.
2. **Never Paywall Critical Hospital Data**: Basic clinic directions, room numbers, OPD timings, and emergency hotlines will remain **100% free and accessible forever**.
3. **Zero Data Brokering**: Patient interactions, search history, and feedback will never be sold to diagnostic centers, insurance brokers, or telemarketers.
4. **Transparent Affiliate Boundaries**: Any external convenience service (stays, transport, equipment) will be clearly labeled as independent third-party resources.

## 3. The 5 Strategic Revenue Streams

### Stream 1: B2B Enterprise White-Label Licensing ("Hospital Saathi SaaS")
* **The Opportunity**: Large private hospital chains (Max Healthcare, Fortis, Apollo, Medanta) and new AIIMS campuses suffer heavy counter congestion, delayed doctor consultations, and patient dissatisfaction due to confusing multi-wing layouts.
* **The Product**: License the PGI Saathi navigation engine as a white-labeled Progressive Web App tailored to their campus layouts, integrated with their internal token management systems.
* **Revenue Model**:
  * Setup & Digital Mapping Fee: ₹2,00,000 – ₹5,00,000 per campus.
  * Annual Software Maintenance Contract (SMC/SaaS): **₹5,00,000 – ₹25,00,000 per year per hospital**.

### Stream 2: Outstation Patient Logistics & Accommodation Marketplace
* **The Opportunity**: Over 60% of PGIMER's 10,000+ daily visitors travel from Punjab, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, and Rajasthan. Their primary out-of-pocket expenses are lodging, transport, and equipment.
* **Services**:
  * **Verified Budget Lodging**: Curated, hygienic guesthouses, budget hotels, and Dharamshalas in Sector 11, Sector 12, Nayagaon, and Sarangpur.
  * **Inter-City Medical Taxis**: Fixed-rate return travel for post-operative patients and cancer chemotherapy visitors.
  * **Medical Equipment Rentals**: Wheelchairs, oxygen concentrators, and walker rentals delivered to the hospital gate.
* **Revenue Model**: Commission-based referral fees (**5% – 10% per booking**).

### Stream 3: On-Ground Patient Attendant Service ("Saathi Buddy")
* **The Opportunity**: Elderly patients, outstation families, and visitors with limited literacy find navigating tokens, counters, and OPD buildings overwhelming.
* **The Service**: A trained, background-checked nursing student or patient attendant meets the patient at Gate 1, helps stand in registration lines, assists with wheelchairs, and guides them between labs and clinics.
* **Revenue Model**:
  * Half-day visit package: ₹800 – ₹1,200.
  * Platform commission / take-rate: **20% – 25% per booking**.

### Stream 4: CSR Grants & Institutional Healthcare Funding
* **The Opportunity**: Under Indian company law, large corporate entities must disburse 2% of their average net profit into verified Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives. Healthcare accessibility and patient welfare are high-priority funding domains.
* **Target Partners**: Public and private sector banks (HDFC Parivartan, SBI Foundation, ICICI Foundation), pharmaceutical manufacturers, and IT firms.
* **Revenue Model**: Annual CSR sponsorships and tech innovation grants (**₹10 Lakh – ₹50 Lakh**).

### Stream 5: External Diagnostic & Home Care Tie-ups
* **The Opportunity**: Routine diagnostic investigations (specialized MRI/CT, rare blood assays) frequently have long waiting queues at public institutions.
* **The Service**: Connecting patients with NABL-accredited diagnostic centers in Chandigarh and Mohali for rapid private testing when the patient opts not to wait.
* **Revenue Model**: Lead generation / referral fee per booked diagnostic appointment.

## 4. Phased Execution Plan

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PHASE 1 (Now – 10k Users)   | Build Trust, Zero Monetization, Maximum Reach   |
| PHASE 2 (10k – 50k Users)   | Launch Logistics & Verified Stays Marketplace   |
| PHASE 3 (50k+ Scale)        | B2B Enterprise SaaS Licensing & CSR Funding     |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Phase 1: Foundation & Word-of-Mouth Fame (0 – 10,000 Active Users)
* **Strategy**: Keep the app **100% free, lighting-fast, and completely clean**.
* **Key Milestones**:
  * Controlled pilot evaluation with initial patient cohorts.
  * Outreach to patient welfare groups, Gurdwara/Temple accommodation managers, and regional patient communities across Punjab, Haryana, and Himachal Pradesh.
  * Gather genuine user testimonials and document real-world waiting time reductions.
* **Revenue**: ₹0 (Focus exclusively on virality, trust, and organic adoption).

### Phase 2: Hyperlocal Logistics & Equipment Marketplace (10,000 – 50,000 Users)
* **Strategy**: Launch the convenience layer for outstation families.
* **Key Milestones**:
  * Onboard 20–30 verified budget guesthouses, dharamshalas, and hotels within 3 km of PGIMER.
  * Partner with local wheelchair and medical equipment rental providers in Chandigarh.
  * Pilot the "Saathi Buddy" attendant booking service for elderly visitors.
* **Projected Revenue**: ₹50,000 – ₹2,00,000 / month.

### Phase 3: Scale, B2B SaaS & CSR Grants (50,000+ Users)
* **Strategy**: Transform proven navigation metrics into enterprise sales and institutional sponsorships.
* **Key Milestones**:
  * Pitch white-label "Hospital Saathi" to private hospital chains (Max, Fortis, Medanta) and new AIIMS facilities.
  * Secure corporate CSR sponsorships for PGIMER patient welfare digitisation.
  * Integrate Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) standard workflows.
* **Projected Revenue**: ₹15 Lakh – ₹50 Lakh / year.
